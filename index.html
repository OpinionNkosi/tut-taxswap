How it works

Three steps, no strangers off-campus

1. Verify your TUT email

Sign up with your @tut4life.ac.za address — every account is a real, checked TUT student.

2. Browse or list

Search for what you need, or list what you no longer use for sale, rent or swap.

3. Trade with confidence

Message the other student directly. AI Support is on hand for questions, and flags anything that looks like a scam.
